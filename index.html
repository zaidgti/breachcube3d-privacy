Privacy Policy for BreachCube 3D

Last updated: November 14, 2026

BreachCube 3D ("the app") is a puzzle game developed and operated by Kelip Labs ("we" or "us"), an independent developer and the developer named on the app's Google Play listing. This policy explains what data the app collects, what it keeps on your phone, and how to reach us.

In short: BreachCube 3D collects no personal data. It has no internet access, no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your progress and settings stay on your phone.

Information We Collect

None. The app doesn't ask for internet access, so it can't send anything to us or to anyone else, and it has no sign-in or account of any kind.

What Stays on Your Phone

The app keeps a few things in its own private storage on your phone, so the game can pick up where you left off:

  • your best star rating for each floor, which also decides which floors are unlocked
  • your sound, haptics and graphics settings
  • the game's sound effects, which it makes itself the first time it runs

None of this is personal information, and none of it leaves your phone through the app. If your phone's own Android backup to your Google account is on, Android may include the app's progress and settings in that backup, which Google keeps for you under its own policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy.

Permissions

  • Vibration: for short haptic feedback when a wall is breached, a flashbang goes off or someone goes down. Turn it off on the main menu with Haptics: Off.

The app asks for no other permissions.

Third-Party Services

None. BreachCube 3D contains no third-party libraries, advertising, analytics or crash reporting tools.

Data Retention and Deletion

Your progress and settings stay on your phone until you clear the app's data or uninstall the app, which deletes them.

Children's Privacy

BreachCube 3D doesn't collect personal data from anyone, including children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time, for example if a later version of the app adds a feature that needs data. Changes will be posted here with an updated "Last updated" date.
